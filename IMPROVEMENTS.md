2. **Improved Lighting**

3. **Skybox Added**
   - Gradient sky from blue to white
   - Atmospheric fog

### What's Working
- Faction selection (Pirates/Marines)
- Character movement with WASD
- Camera rotation with Q/E or right-click drag
- Combat with SPACE or left-click
- Fruit moves with Z/X/C/V keys
- Shop, Inventory, Quests, Stats menus
- Enemy spawning and AI
- Level progression and mastery system

### Controls
- **WASD** - Move (camera-relative)
- **SPACE** - Melee attack
- **Z/X/C/V** - Fruit moves
- **Q/E** - Rotate camera
- **Right-click + Drag** - Camera control
- **M** - Map
- **I** - Inventory
- **B** - Shop
- **T** - Stats
- **Scroll** or Zoom buttons - Zoom in/out

## How to Play
1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
2. Select Pirates or Marines faction
3. Use WASD to move around the island
4. Press B to open the shop and buy fruits
5. Use Z/X/C/V to use fruit abilities
6. Fight enemies to gain EXP and level up
7. Complete quests for rewards
8. Explore different islands as you level up

The game is now much more realistic with proper Roblox-style graphics, smooth animations, and authentic gameplay mechanics!
